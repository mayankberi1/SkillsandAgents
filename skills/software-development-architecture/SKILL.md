# software-development-architecture

## Activation and inputs

Evaluate boundaries, dependency direction and architectural alternatives. Use YAGNI principals and be concise and minimalist. 
Inputs: Approved requirements and existing architecture/code. 
Read applicable central AGENTS.md; this capability grants no additional authorisation.
use domain engineering, SOLID and YAGNI principals.

## Procedure

Before analysis, identify the material choices and ask the user focused questions. Offer options and recommendations. Brainstorm alternatives and failure scenarios with the user before recommending a design. Do not substitute assumed requirements for missing answers.
Analyse boundaries, dependency direction, reuse, technology, patterns, security/scalability and drift; compare at least two credible material alternatives.
Discuss consequential choices with the user under the shared questioning policy. Clarify scale, reliability, security/privacy, deployment, existing integrations, operational ownership, budget and technology constraints as applicable. Brainstorm credible alternatives and concrete failure scenarios; make trade-offs understandable and confirm priorities before recommending a material decision.

Trace components, data flow and reuse. Compare at least two credible alternatives for material choices across complexity, security, scalability and ownership. Check dependency direction and cycles. Evaluate patterns and SOLID pragmatically. Document significant decisions and aligned Mermaid component diagrams. Present the result and wait for approval before the next stage.

## Outputs and boundaries

Architecture proposal, alternatives and constraints. 
List new and modified components, assets, APIs, Microservices, tech stack, layers, inscope, architecure patterns, and out of scope architecture.
List impacted components and files (new or existing).
Create and maintain a architecture diagram. Split the diagram in parts if it becomes too big.
use domain engineering, YAGNI, and SOLID principals.
