# requirements-analysis

## Activation and inputs

Clarify requirements, scope and acceptance criteria before design. Relevant roles: Product Owner, Coordinator. Inputs: User goals, existing product records and constraints. Read applicable central AGENTS.md; this capability grants no additional authorisation.

## Procedure

Apply the shared collaborative discovery policy. Interview the user in focused rounds about users, goals, examples, business rules, priorities, constraints and success/failure cases; follow up until material ambiguity is resolved. Brainstorm candidate workflows when useful, then separate confirmed needs from exploratory ideas and seek approval of the detailed proposal.

Read relevant product/code evidence. Separate confirmed needs from assumptions. Assign requirement IDs; define scope/exclusions, stories where useful and observable positive/negative acceptance criteria. Propose priorities and backlog additions without changing existing entries. Resolve conflicts and present a concrete revision for requirements approval.

For `/backlog`, inspect project evidence and research external sources before suggesting features and asking focused questions; cite evidence and disclose limitations. For `/plan`, match the saved backlog first and persist new directly requested items through Coordinator before requirements work. Follow the shared product backlog contract in agent-planning; discovery approval is separate from requirements approval.

## Outputs and boundaries

Requirements proposal; criteria; open questions; approval target. Include decisions, deliverable references, evidence, risks/blockers and next action. Feeds iteration-planning and design; does not select architecture.
