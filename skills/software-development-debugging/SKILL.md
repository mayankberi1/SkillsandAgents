# software-development-debugging

## Activation and inputs

Diagnose defects and reconcile interrupted workflows safely. Inputs: Failure evidence or iteration record, actual file/session state. Read applicable central AGENTS.md; this capability grants no additional authorisation.

## Procedure

For defects reproduce, isolate cause and test hypotheses; change only approved scope and prove remediation/regression. Bound attempts to central policy limit. For interruption read records, inspect file/session state, compare actual progress and identify inconsistencies. Present reconstruction and obtain confirmation before continuing. Pause records in-flight work without destructive cleanup.

## Outputs and boundaries

Diagnosis/remediation evidence or recovery proposal. Include decisions, deliverable references, evidence, risks/blockers and next action. Use software-development-coding for approved fixes. Recovery never invents missing approval.
