# architecture

## Activation and inputs

Evaluate boundaries, dependency direction and architectural alternatives. Relevant roles: Architect, Solution Designer, Code Reviewer. Inputs: Approved requirements and existing architecture/code. Read applicable central AGENTS.md; this capability grants no additional authorisation.

## Procedure

Apply the shared collaborative discovery policy to material constraints and decisions. Ask about operational needs and priorities, brainstorm alternatives with the user and explore failure scenarios before recommending a design. Do not substitute assumed requirements for missing answers.

Trace components/data flow and reuse. Compare at least two credible alternatives for material choices across complexity, security, scalability and ownership. Check dependency direction/cycles. Evaluate patterns and SOLID pragmatically. Document significant decisions and aligned Mermaid component diagrams; seek material approval.

## Outputs and boundaries

Architecture proposal, alternatives and constraints. Include decisions, deliverable references, evidence, risks/blockers and next action. Dependency-assessment supplies library trade-offs; solution-design details contracts.
